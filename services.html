// COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything business owners ask before starting.

Do I or my team need any technical coding skills?

Not at all. Everything we build is designed for normal non-technical people. You interact with chatbots via familiar apps like WhatsApp or standard web buttons, and dashboards look just like clean visual reports.

How does the chatbot know my company's specific rules?

We provide the chatbot with your exact business guides, service descriptions, pricing policies, and FAQs. It only answers questions based on your provided material, ensuring 100% accurate responses.

How long does a typical setup take to go live?

Most custom chatbots, voice bots, and invoice workflows launch within 1 to 2 weeks for rapid setups, or 3 to 4 weeks for deep multi-system company-wide integrations.

What happens if a customer asks something unusual?

The bot politely lets the customer know that it is connecting them with a human specialist, and immediately alerts you or your team member on WhatsApp with the customer's question and contact number.
